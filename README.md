PRACTICAL PIGS

This datapack does a few things. A few more things to come soon.

1) Pigs now spawn a few extra piglets when bred, upwards of 4 at most.
2) Pigs drop a retextured and disabled salmon spawn egg called "tallow".
3) Said Tallow can be used to make candles (2), torches (8, a great deal!), and an intermediary item with which to make charcoal (2)!
3) you can throw regular raw porkchops and cooked porkchops in the blasting furnace to get yourself a little extra charcoal too!

Please drop a copy of this datapack both in your resource folder and your datapack folder to get the most out of this project, thanks. :)
